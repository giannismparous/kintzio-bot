Services for your company • Konstantinos Kintzios

Empowering individuals and organizations to unlock the true wealth beyond KPIs and processes—where metrics align with values and human potential thrives.

Everything you’ve achieved so far is just a part of what you’re truly capable of. Now is the time to step up and move to the next level.

Your company is its people.

Your company’s value will always reflect the value you place on your people.

I’m not for everyone – and that’s perfectly fine.

If you’re seeking something unique, a partner who brings depth, genuine connection, and meaningful change, then you’re in the right place. The experiences I craft are designed to inspire, provoke thought, and drive real impact – not to simply fill a slot or check a box.

Take a moment to explore the services I offer. If my approach resonates with you, let’s connect and bring it to life.

Business Consulting

The success of a business doesn’t lie in magical formulas or copy-paste strategies. It lies in the ability to see clearly, make decisions with substance, and remain adaptable in an ever-changing world.

Diagnosis: We start at the root of your challenges, not the symptoms. What’s blocking your growth, efficiency, or company culture?

Planning: Together, we create a plan that doesn’t sit on paper but gets implemented and delivers results. A plan tailored specifically to your needs.

Execution: I support you in action. Whether it’s managing change, strengthening teams, or upgrading your strategy, I’m here to ensure the plan becomes a reality.

If you believe it’s time to clarify your direction, resolve persistent inefficiencies, or transition from “operational survival” to “strategic growth,” we can start there.

Keynote Speaking Engagements

If you’re looking for someone to step on stage, deliver the same clichés for 1-2 hours with a prepackaged presentation, get a round of applause, and walk away – I’m not the speaker for you.

I don’t do keynote speeches the way you might be used to. I lead dynamic, raw, interactive discussions. In the U.S., they call them powerful discussions and fireside chats. In reality, they are something much more important: real conversations with real people about real problems.

This format creates a warm, engaging atmosphere, where the audience doesn’t just listen – they participate. People are encouraged to ask questions, share thoughts, and become part of the conversation from the very first minute.

I don’t present theories. I provide answers.

I don’t speak in concepts. I speak in real experiences.

I don’t step on stage just to inspire—that’s the easy part.

I step on stage to challenge perspectives and spark productive reflection—and that’s the hard part.

Every company, every team, every organization has its own set of questions:

“Why aren’t we succeeding?”

“Why don’t we communicate effectively?”

“Why aren’t our people giving 100%?”

These questions aren’t answered with scripted presentations and surface-level motivation filled with empty slogans.

They are answered through real discussion, honest dialogue, and uncomfortable truths that push people forward.

I offer around 50 thematic pillars, covering everything that could concern a professional, a team, or an entire company. And if you don’t find exactly what you need, we’ll create a new one together.

Because the conversation isn’t about what I have to say. It’s about what you need to hear.

If you’re looking for something standard and predictable, there are plenty of other options.

But if you want your team to walk out of the room different from how they walked in – then we should talk.

Executive and C-Level Mentoring

Offering specialized mentoring services designed specifically for high-level executives, focusing on enhancing strategic leadership and decision-making skills.

These sessions are tailored to align closely with organizational goals and values, ensuring that leadership actions are both effective and reflective of company culture. This approach fosters a deeper understanding of complex business dynamics and supports sustained executive growth.

Generation Bridge Program

Providing practical guidance and mentoring for organizations aiming to enhance engagement with Gen Z employees, this program includes workshops and coaching sessions that delve into Gen Z’s unique values, communication styles, and workplace expectations.

According to a Deloitte survey, 44% of Gen Z employees believe their work expectations differ significantly from their older counterparts, highlighting the need for tailored engagement strategies. This service emphasizes techniques for motivating and integrating younger employees into multi-generational teams, promoting a harmonious and productive work environment that leverages the unique strengths of each generation.

Through these efforts, organizations can effectively address the discrepancies and misalignments that often exist between Gen Z and other workplace generations.

Strategic Connection Facilitation

Offering expert facilitation services to connect individuals and organizations with the ideal partners, collaborators, or service providers.

Leveraging a vast network and deep understanding of diverse needs, this service efficiently matches clients with the right resources and individuals. By prioritizing human-centric understanding and clear communication, I ensure that each connection not only meets specific requirements but also fosters long-term, beneficial relationships.

This service is ideal for anyone looking to expand their reach or fulfill specific project needs with precision and personal insight.

Leadership Development Workshops

Conducting interactive workshops and development talks for teams and leaders, focusing on practical leadership skills and human-centric management strategies.

Offering both large-group sessions and intimate workshops to ensure personalized attention and engagement.

Personal Development Talks

Delivering engaging and motivational talks on personal development themes, empowering individuals to actively pursue their professional and personal goals.

Ensuring each session provides actionable insights and strategies that attendees can apply immediately in their personal and professional lives.

People Management Skills Development

Enhancing the people management capabilities across all levels of your organization, from team members to top executives, this comprehensive training program is designed to cultivate essential skills such as empathetic leadership, effective communication, and conflict resolution. Through interactive sessions and real-world applications, participants learn to navigate diverse team dynamics and drive performance while prioritizing the human element of the workplace.

Supporting a thriving corporate culture that values personal growth and cohesive team collaboration, this service ensures every level of management is equipped to lead with humanity and insight.

Executive Public Speaking Training

Providing specialized public speaking training for executives, focusing on enhancing communication skills for leadership roles. Emphasizing the mastery of persuasive speaking, crisis communication, and stakeholder engagement to help leaders articulate their vision and influence both internal and external audiences effectively.

This training also stresses the importance of human-centricity and building genuine connections, ensuring leaders can foster empathy and engagement within their teams and broader networks.

Team Building

From team-building games and cocktail workshops to wellness experiences, we craft unique corporate events designed to energize teams, foster creativity, and build connections.

Whether it’s an icebreaker for new teams or a tailored gift box to celebrate achievements, we ensure every detail is fun, interactive, and impactful. Let’s create memorable moments that leave lasting impressions – because work is meant to be enjoyed!
