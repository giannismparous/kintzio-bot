Every number below is from his own site or from live output of the working build.
Nothing here is aspirational.

---

> **500+** keynotes. **250+** companies. **8,000+** professionals mentored.
> **400+** podcast episodes.
>
> And every single enquiry still lands in an inbox, to be answered by you.

## 3. The guarantee — the actual differentiator

International tools are English-first. Coachvox translates in and out, so a Greek
answer is an English answer wearing Greek. Ours is native GR/EN with no
translation step, and **language is a first-class field on every chunk**.

But the real differentiator is narrower and more valuable to him:

> **It can only say things you have actually said.**
>
> Not "it tries not to make things up." It is *structurally unable* to. Every
> quote is checked, word by word, against your real material before it reaches
> the screen. If a quote cannot be verified, it is deleted, not flagged.

For a man whose entire brand is authenticity, a fabricated quote in his voice is
the one catastrophic failure. This is engineered against, and it is tested:
**120 automated tests**, including tests that deliberately break the safety rules
to confirm the alarms fire.

And the co-created-podcast problem, which he will raise:

> «Θα Σας Ειδοποιήσουμε» is yours *and* Spyros's. The system tags every sentence
> with who said it and whether it is cleared for publication. Guest material can
> power your team's internal search while being **structurally barred** from
> public answers. Live index right now: **156 of 158** chunks cleared,
> **2** internal-only, **154** eligible to speak as you.

## 4. What he gets, in his terms

1. **Digital Kitsi** — answers as him, in GR or EN, citing him. Named *Kitsi*
   deliberately: a diminutive **of** Kintzios, never impersonating him. Legally
   required, and better manners.
2. **The pillar navigator** — someone describes a problem, gets the thematic
   pillars that fit. This is the one feature his site is missing today.
3. **A 24/7 qualifier** — four flows built from his own contact form: keynote,
   workshop/consulting, mentoring, and *guest on the show*. It asks the questions
   he would ask, then hands over a briefed lead. It never quotes a price.
4. **Internal search over 400 episodes** — "every time I talked about quiet
   quitting," with timestamps. For him and his team only.

## 5. The three asks (be blunt — he will respect it)

1. **The ~50 pillar titles**, one paragraph each. Currently placeholders; this is
   the single highest-leverage thing he can hand over.
2. **20 episodes to start**, with permission — not 400. A calibration set proves
   voice fidelity before anyone invests in the full archive.
3. **Written sign-off from Spyros Andrianos** on what may be used publicly.
   *No co-created audio is ingested until this exists.*

Framing that lands: *"I would rather show you 20 episodes done properly than
promise you 400."*

## 6. Commercials

**Setup €6,000–12,000 + €400–900/month.**

Justify it from his own economics, not from cost:
* One booked keynote covers the setup fee.
* Published agency rates for custom digital twins start at $10,000–15,000 and
  run to $75,000–150,000+. Delphi is $99–349/mo and Coachvox $99/mo — both
  English-first, self-serve, no compliance posture, no Greek.
* The monthly buys something that arrives every month: a report of **what your
  audience asked that you have not published on**. That is a content calendar
  derived from real demand, not a hosting invoice.

## 7. Two things to say out loud

Say them before he finds them. Both build credibility:

* **"The persona voice has not been signed off yet."** Retrieval, guardrails,
  quote verification, lead capture and GDPR erasure are all built and tested. But
  *you* must read twenty answers and tell us they sound like you. Nobody else can
  approve that.
* **"Your privacy notice does not cover this."** His current policy covers a
  contact form and says comments are kept indefinitely. A chat assistant that
  stores conversations and leads needs its own notice, reviewed by his lawyer.
  We built the erasure endpoint and a 180-day retention default; the legal text
  is his side.

## What NOT to promise

* **Voice cloning.** Not yet, not without written rights and a settled marking
  standard under the AI Act. Offer the stronger version instead: click a citation
  and **hear the actual 20 seconds of him saying it.** Real audio, not synthetic.
* **A booking that closes itself.** It qualifies and hands over. He closes.
* **"It knows everything you've said."** It knows what has been ingested and
  cleared. Today that is his website plus two sample transcripts.
